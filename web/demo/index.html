Narduk Sound on the web

KZFR 90.1 FM, live, through the NardukSound visualizers ported from Metal to WebGL2.

Keys: ← → change the look, F full screen, Space pause.

A URL plays only if its server sends Access-Control-Allow-Origin; without it the sound plays but the picture stays dark. Files never leave your device. Flashes are limited to three a second, and with reduced motion turned on the visualizer shows a still picture. Source: narduk-sound/web.
